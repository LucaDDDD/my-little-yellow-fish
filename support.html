FAQ

How do I move the fish?

Tilt your phone — up, down, left, right. The fish follows the tilt. Hold the phone the way you find comfortable during the 3-2-1 countdown: that pose becomes your neutral position, every time.

The controls feel off.

Pause and resume (or restart): the countdown recalibrates the tilt to however you're holding the phone right now.

What's the golden bar?

Every bite charges it. When it's full: GNAM TIME — for eight seconds everything is edible and every bite is worth ×5.

The leaderboard says I'm not signed in.

The global leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center. Sign in from your iPhone's Settings → Games (or Game Center), then reopen the game.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Sound: the speaker button on the home screen mutes everything. Vibration: gear button → Settings → Vibration.
