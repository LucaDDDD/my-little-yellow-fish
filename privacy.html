Privacy Policy

My Little Yellow Fish

Effective date: June 11, 2026

The short version

My Little Yellow Fish collects no personal data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps a few things locally so it works the way you left it: your best score, the audio mute switch and the vibration setting. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game submits your score to the global leaderboard and shows the top players' public nicknames and scores. This is handled entirely by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We never see, store or process this data on any server of ours — the game has no servers at all. If you are not signed in to Game Center, the game works normally and nothing is shared.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected, no data is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the game handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that update ships.
